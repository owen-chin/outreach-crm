# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Anyone running sponsorship or partnership outreach for events — not just the
original builder. Each user signs in with their own Google account and works
entirely within their own data: their projects, their organizations, their
Gmail. The app is built to be handed to other organizers and small teams doing
the same kind of tiered outreach (sponsors, partners, donors), not just to
serve a single personal use case. The job to be done, on any given screen, is
moving a specific organization forward: draft or reply to an email, check
where a thread stands, or see which orgs across a project need attention next.

## Product Purpose

Longlist is a lightweight CRM for managing sponsorship and partnership
outreach campaigns, organized as Project → Category → Organization →
People/Threads (e.g. Project = an event, Category = a sponsor tier,
Organization = a company being pitched). It sends and replies through the
user's own Gmail account via OAuth rather than a separate SMTP tool, applies
Gmail labels automatically, and includes an AI chat assistant for drafting
that reads full thread history on replies. Success is outreach conducted at
volume without losing track of any single organization or thread.

## Positioning

Longlist sends real email from your own Gmail account — OAuth-connected,
auto-labeled, with AI-assisted drafting built in — rather than asking you to
adopt a separate outbound tool or a CRM that lives apart from your actual
inbox.

## Brand Personality

Crisp, fast, focused. The tool should feel efficient and get out of the way —
optimized for moving quickly through a lot of organizations and threads, not
for making a visual impression. Explicitly not a cutesy consumer app: no
illustration, mascot, or playful gradient/emoji treatments that would
undercut the sense of a sharp, professional tool a solo organizer or small
team can rely on.

## Anti-references

Cutesy, illustration-heavy consumer app styling — playful gradients, mascots,
emoji-driven UI. Longlist is a working tool, not a delight-first product.

## Design Principles

Speed over ceremony — every flow should minimize the distance between
"see an organization" and "send or reply," since the core value is outreach
at volume.

Your Gmail, not a walled garden — the app should always read as a thin,
trustworthy layer over the user's real inbox, never as a separate system
where data or messages get locked away.

Crisp density over enterprise bloat — favor compact, information-dense views
(kanban board, inbox, dashboard) over generic SaaS card grids and dashboard
chrome. This is a tool for one person or a small team, not enterprise
software.

Never cutesy — keep the visual tone sharp and utilitarian, in line with the
brand personality above.

Status at a glance — dashboard and board views exist to answer "where does
everything stand" instantly across many concurrent projects and
organizations.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast standards throughout. Status is currently communicated
by badge/dot color alone across six states (not_contacted, contacted,
responded, negotiating, confirmed, declined) — this is an accepted tradeoff
for now, not a requirement to fix as part of every design pass.
